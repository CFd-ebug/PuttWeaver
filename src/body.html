          Live ball tracking

          Camera view. Tap the ball here to sample its color.

          1 · Camera

          2 · Fit the projection (angled projector)

          Skip this if the projector points straight at the sheet. Otherwise drag the four corners until the test card’s squares are true squares on the floor.

          3 · Calibrate

          Projects 13 dots one at a time, then checks itself with 6 more. Takes about 20 seconds; keep the camera still.

          4 · Ball

          Put the ball in the ring that appears on the right of the sheet, then press Sample ball.

          Compare to empty sheet photographs the bare sheet at the start of each round, then looks for something that soaks up blue light the way a yellow ball does. Skin, hair and shadows don’t, so hands and heads are ignored. Lower the blue limit if anything else gets picked up; raise it if the ball is missed.

          Color-only settings

          5 · Play

          Movement guard: while more than this share of the sheet is changing (someone walking on it, a hand reaching in), no shot can start and the next round waits. The frame-rate overlay (F) shows the live figure.

          After each shot the player walks in and picks up the ball. Once the sheet has been clear for a moment, the next round starts by itself. N forces the next round; C cancels a false start.

              Surface speed

              Stimp rating: how many feet a ball rolls after leaving a stimpmeter. Putting turf runs about 10. Smooth plastic over a hard floor will run faster, so measure yours.

              Sheet flatness

              Adds hidden bumps and a small floor tilt, so slow balls drift near the end of a roll. Each change lays a new random sheet.

              A mouse is far more precise than a putter, so every putt gets random aim and distance error.
